Is Triku Legal?

Triku is a free-to-play entertainment app. You never pay money to take part, and you do not stake any money.

Free to play, no money at stake

  • There is no entry fee for any Contest. You may be asked to watch a short advertisement before entry; watching it is free and is not a payment to us.
  • Triku does not accept deposits, wallet top-ups or purchases of any kind.
  • Every Reward is paid by Perifury Private Limited from its own budget, which may be supported by advertising or sponsorship income. No Reward comes from money collected from players.

How Triku fits Indian law

The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 prohibits "online money games". These are games played by paying fees, depositing money or other stakes in the expectation of winning money or other enrichment.

Triku is designed so that no player pays a fee, deposits money or puts money or any other stake at risk. On this basis, we consider that Triku is not an online money game. We will follow any determination, registration requirement or guidance issued under the Act, including by the Online Gaming Authority of India, and will update Triku and this page if needed.

No betting on Triku

You cannot place a bet or wager on Triku. Our Terms of Use prohibit using Triku Contests or results as the basis for betting with anyone else.

Lucky Winner

Some Contests pick one extra winner at random from the players who answered correctly. Entry is free, and no one pays, or can pay, money for a chance to be selected.

Who can play

Triku is only for people aged 18 and over who live in India.

Tax on Rewards

Rewards are taxable. Where the Income-tax Act, 2025 requires, we deduct tax at source (currently 30%) from Rewards and issue TDS certificates.

Play for fun

Triku is meant to be entertainment. There are no in-app purchases, and you never need to spend money to keep playing.
