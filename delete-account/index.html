Delete your Triku account.

Triku is operated by Perifury Private Limited.

You can request deletion here without installing or signing in to the app. In Triku, open More → Delete account.

Once accepted, your account is locked and all sessions are revoked. Your profile, location, device and other non-retained account data will be removed through our cleanup process. This is not just account suspension. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

We aim to complete ordinary cleanup within 30 days. If you have a wallet balance, pending withdrawals or unsettled contests, we accept the request and work with you to resolve these before final deletion. We do not automatically forfeit your money. We will explain any delay through support.

KYC, tax, payout and financial records remain protected for applicable compliance and dispute-resolution purposes. They are excluded from automatic deletion. We will provide retention information on request at grievance@perifury.com.
