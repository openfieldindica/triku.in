Privacy Policy.

Triku app and related services
Effective date: 5 November 2026

Triku is operated by Perifury Private Limited ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you use the Triku app and related services. It should be read with our Terms of Use.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we collect:

  • Account information: mobile number, username, profile details, age-eligibility confirmation, language and communication preferences, and information used to authenticate your account.
  • Participation information: predictions and poll responses, contest entries, results and account activity.
  • Verification and transaction information: name, Aadhaar verification information, age-verification details, PAN, bank-account information, verification outcomes, wallet and withdrawal records, and information required for tax compliance.
  • Technical and usage information: IP address, device and app details, installation and session identifiers, notification tokens, interactions with the app and advertisements, and diagnostic information such as crash reports.
  • Location information: approximate location to check eligibility and prevent misuse. Where device permission is needed, we request it before accessing location. We do not use this feature for precise-location collection or continuous live-location tracking.
  • Support information: information you provide when contacting us, including messages and attachments.

Information may be provided by you, generated through your use of Triku, or received from service providers involved in authentication, verification and transactions.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate and secure your account; administer contests and results; verify eligibility and identity; process withdrawals and applicable taxes; respond to enquiries; send service communications; prevent fraud and misuse; and comply with legal obligations.

We also use usage and diagnostic information to understand participation and repeat use, improve the app and resolve technical problems. Optional promotional communications are subject to your preferences and any required consent.

3. Analytics and advertising

We use services including Google Firebase for analytics, diagnostics, notifications and app configuration. These services process usage, device and technical information for their respective functions. Analytics may be associated with internal account identifiers.

Where advertising is enabled, providers including Google AdMob may process advertising and device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure advertisements and prevent invalid activity. We obtain consent where required. We do not provide identity documents, OTPs or bank-account details for advertising targeting.

4. How we share information

We share information as needed with hosting and infrastructure providers; authentication and messaging providers; identity-verification providers, payment processors and banks; analytics, diagnostics and advertising providers; and authorised support personnel and professional advisers. Access is limited to the relevant purpose and subject to appropriate safeguards.

We may disclose information when required by law or lawful process, or where lawfully necessary to prevent fraud, address security incidents or protect rights. If Triku undergoes a merger, acquisition or business transfer, personal information may be transferred with appropriate safeguards and any notice or consent required by law.

Contest features may display your username, rank and results to other users. We do not publicly disclose your private bank details or government identification numbers. We do not sell personal or sensitive user data.

5. Your choices and account deletion

You can manage communication preferences in the app and device permissions, including location and notifications, in your device settings. You may contact us to request access to or correction of your information, withdraw consent where processing depends on it, or raise a privacy concern. Withholding information or withdrawing consent may affect features that need that information.

Account deletion in the app and a dedicated account-deletion page are coming soon. For now, you can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing grievance@perifury.com. We may take reasonable steps to verify that the request comes from you. Uninstalling Triku does not delete your account.

We delete account-associated information on a verified deletion request, except information retained for legitimate purposes described below. Account suspension is not a substitute for deletion.

6. Retention and security

We retain personal information for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Verification, transaction, tax and relevant security or dispute records may be retained after account deletion where needed for legal compliance, fraud prevention or resolving claims. Retained records are restricted to those purposes and deleted or anonymised when no longer required. Backup copies are removed through the applicable backup-expiry process.

We use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Where service providers process information outside India, we apply safeguards required by applicable law.

7. Age eligibility and policy changes

Triku is intended for adults aged 18 or older. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Perifury Private Limited may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, data practices or applicable law. The revised policy will be published with its effective date. We will provide notice of material changes and obtain fresh consent where required by applicable law. Changes will not limit your statutory rights.

8. Contact us

For privacy enquiries, corrections, consent withdrawal or deletion requests: grievance@perifury.com.
